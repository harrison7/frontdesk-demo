# Frequently Asked Questions

<!-- SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA: fictional business. -->

## Do you offer free estimates?

Estimates for replacing a furnace, air conditioner, heat pump, or water heater are free. Repair visits use the standard $89 diagnostic fee, which is credited toward the repair if you approve it during the same visit.

## Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes. In this fictional demo, every technician is described as licensed, insured, and background-checked. No real license numbers are published here because the business is not real.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit and debit cards, checks, and bank transfers (ACH). Payment is due when the work is completed. Financing is available on installations over $2,000.

## What brands do you service?

We service and repair all major brands of furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, and water heaters, and we install equipment from several manufacturers.

## Do you offer senior or military discounts?

Yes. Customers 65 or older and active-duty military members or veterans receive 10% off repairs. This discount cannot be combined with Comfort Club plan discounts.

## How often should I change my furnace filter?

Most homes should replace a standard 1-inch furnace filter every 1 to 3 months, and more often with pets or during heavy-use seasons.

## Should I repair or replace my system?

As a rule of thumb, if your system is more than 15 years old and a repair would cost more than about one-third of a replacement, replacement is often worth considering. Your technician will give you both options in writing so you can decide.

## Do you provide do-it-yourself repair instructions?

For safety reasons we do not give instructions for gas line, gas appliance, or electrical work. For simple maintenance like changing a furnace filter, we are happy to explain.

## Will the technician clean up afterward?

Yes. Technicians wear shoe covers inside your home and clean up the work area before leaving.

## Do I need to be home for the appointment?

Yes, an adult 18 or older needs to be home for the visit. Please secure pets before the technician arrives.
