# Maintenance Plans (Comfort Club)

<!-- SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA: fictional business, invented prices. -->

## Comfort Club Basic

- Comfort Club Basic price: $14 per month, or $159 per year when paid annually.
- Includes one furnace tune-up and one air conditioner tune-up each year.
- Includes priority scheduling ahead of non-members.
- Includes a waived diagnostic fee on repair visits.
- Includes 10% off repairs.

## Comfort Club Plus

- Comfort Club Plus price: $24 per month, or $269 per year when paid annually.
- Includes everything in Comfort Club Basic.
- Adds one water heater flush and one whole-home plumbing inspection each year.
- Includes 15% off repairs instead of 10%.
- Waives the after-hours emergency dispatch fee.

## Plan details

Each plan covers one heating system and one cooling system at one address. Each additional system can be added for $7 per month. Plan discounts apply to labor and parts on repairs, but not to new system installations. Plan discounts cannot be combined with the senior or military discount.

## Canceling a plan

You can cancel any time with 30 days of notice. Annual plans are refunded for the unused months, minus the regular price of any tune-ups you already received.
