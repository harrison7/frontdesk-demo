# Service Area

## Core service area

Our core service area is Provo, Orem, and Lehi, Utah. There is no trip fee anywhere in our core service area.

## Extended service area

We also serve American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Vineyard, and Saratoga Springs. Visits to the extended service area include a $49 trip fee. Emergency arrival targets are longer in the extended area (see the Emergency Policy document).

## Areas we do not serve

We do not currently serve Salt Lake County, Spanish Fork or cities south of it, Eagle Mountain, Park City, or Heber. If you are outside our area, we suggest searching for a licensed local contractor.

## Types of properties

We serve single-family homes, townhomes, condos, and small offices. We do not take on large commercial buildings or new-construction plumbing for builders.
