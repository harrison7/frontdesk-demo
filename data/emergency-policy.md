## What counts as an emergency

We treat these as emergencies and dispatch 24/7: no heat when it is below 40°F outside, a burst pipe or active leak, sewage backing up into the home, a leaking water heater, or no running water in the home.

## How to reach us in an emergency

Call our 24/7 emergency line at (801) 555-0199. Please call rather than email or chat, so we can dispatch the on-call technician right away.

## Emergency arrival times

We aim to arrive within 2 hours in our core service area (Provo, Orem, and Lehi) and within 3 hours in the extended service area. Arrival times can be longer during severe weather.

## Emergency fees

Emergency visits after 6:00 p.m., on weekends, or on holidays include a $149 after-hours dispatch fee in addition to the repair price. Comfort Club Plus members do not pay the after-hours dispatch fee.

## If you smell gas

If you smell gas (often described as a rotten-egg smell), leave the building immediately with everyone in it. Do not use light switches, phones, or anything that could make a spark while inside. Once you are safely away from the building, call 911 or your gas utility's emergency line. We do not respond to suspected gas leaks first; after the utility or fire department says the home is safe, we can repair or replace the appliance involved.

## If your carbon monoxide alarm goes off

If a carbon monoxide (CO) alarm sounds, get everyone outside into fresh air right away and call 911. Do not go back inside until emergency responders say it is safe. Afterward, we can inspect your furnace, water heater, and venting to find the source; the standard $89 diagnostic fee applies.

## If water is flooding your home

If water is flooding your home, shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely (it is often near the water heater, in the basement, or where the water line enters the house). Stay out of standing water that may be near outlets or electrical equipment. Then call our 24/7 emergency line at (801) 555-0199.
