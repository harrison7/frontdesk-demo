## How our pricing works

Every price on this page is an estimated range for a typical home. Your technician gives you a written, flat-rate price on-site before any work begins, and we never start work without your approval. Ranges include labor and standard parts unless a line says otherwise.

## Service call and diagnostic fee

- Diagnostic fee (any HVAC or plumbing visit): $89.
- The $89 diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair if you approve the work during the same visit.
- After-hours emergency dispatch fee (nights after 6:00 p.m., weekends, and holidays): $149, in addition to the repair price.
- Extended service area trip fee: $49 (see the Service Area document).

## Heating and cooling repairs and tune-ups

- Furnace tune-up: $109.
- Air conditioner tune-up: $109.
- Furnace repair: typically $150 to $650, depending on the part.
- Air conditioner repair: typically $150 to $900, depending on the part.
- Refrigerant recharge: priced per pound after diagnosis, because the amount needed varies by system.
- Smart thermostat installation: $175 to $350.

## Heating and cooling system installation

- New gas furnace, installed: $4,200 to $7,500.
- New central air conditioner, installed: $5,500 to $9,800.
- New heat pump system, installed: $7,000 to $13,500.
- Ductless mini-split (single zone), installed: $3,800 to $6,200.
- Replacement estimates for new systems are free.

## Water heaters

- Tank water heater replacement (40 to 50 gallon), installed: $1,600 to $2,600.
- Tankless water heater installation: $3,200 to $5,400.
- Water heater flush and inspection: $119.
- Water heater repair (thermostat, element, or valve): typically $150 to $450.

## Drains, leaks, and fixtures

- Drain clearing (single sink, tub, or shower): $129 to $249.
- Main sewer line clearing: $249 to $449.
- Sewer camera inspection: $199.
- Leak repair (accessible pipe): $175 to $600.
- Toilet repair: $125 to $295.
- Toilet installation: $295 to $495, plus the cost of the toilet.
- Faucet replacement: $175 to $325, plus the cost of the faucet.
- Garbage disposal replacement, installed: $295 to $495.
- Water softener installation: $1,800 to $3,200.
- Whole-home repiping: quoted only after an in-person inspection.

## Financing

Financing is available on installations over $2,000 through a third-party lending partner, on approved credit. Your comfort advisor can explain current terms during the free replacement estimate.

## Services we do not offer

We do not offer electrical panel work, roofing, appliance repair (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens), septic tank pumping, well pump service, or commercial refrigeration. For those, please contact a specialist in that trade.
