## Workmanship warranty

Our labor (workmanship) warranty covers repairs for 1 year and new installations for 2 years from the date of service.

## Parts and equipment warranty

Parts and new equipment are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, which is typically 5 to 10 years for major components when the equipment is registered. We register new equipment with the manufacturer for you at no charge.

## 30-day repair guarantee

If the same repair fails within 30 days, we come back and fix it at no charge, including the diagnostic fee.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover damage from freezing, power surges, flooding, misuse, or do-it-yourself changes made after our visit. For fixtures or parts that the customer supplies, our labor warranty is 30 days and the part itself is not covered by us.

Call the office at (801) 555-0142 during office hours. There is no diagnostic fee for a warranty visit.
